### Task 3: Create the /help page with accordion content

**Files:**
- Create: `src/app/(app)/help/page.tsx`

**Step 1: Create the help page**

Create `src/app/(app)/help/page.tsx` with:
- Page title "Help & Documentation"
- Brief intro paragraph
- `Accordion` component with `type="multiple"` and `defaultValue` set to all section values
- 8 `AccordionItem` sections with content:

**Sections:**

1. **Getting Started** — DoIt is your calm daily task manager. Sign in with Google to get started. Use the sidebar (desktop) or bottom nav (mobile) to navigate between views.

2. **Today View** — Your daily task list. Shows tasks assigned to today. Mark tasks done with the checkbox, skip with the skip button. The suggestions area shows unassigned tasks you can pull into today. Click the pencil icon to edit a task's details.

3. **Tasks & Backlog** — View all your tasks organized by status. Create new tasks with the + button. Each task can have a name, description, priority (1-3), time estimate, due date, and project. Tasks without a day assigned live in your backlog until you assign or auto-assign them.

4. **Projects** — Group related tasks under projects. Create a project, then add tasks to it. The progress bar shows completion percentage. Click a project to see its tasks and add new ones.

5. **Recurring Tasks** — Set up tasks that repeat on a schedule. Go to Settings to create recurring tasks with daily, weekday, weekly, biweekly, or monthly frequency. Instances are auto-generated for upcoming days. Click a recurring task row to edit it.

6. **Daily Digest** — Get a morning email with your tasks for the day and weather forecast. Enable in Settings under "Daily Digest". Enter your city name and verify it to get weather data. Emails are sent at 8 AM MST.

7. **Schedule & Calendar** — The timeline view shows your day visually. Connect Google Calendar in Settings to see your events alongside tasks. Use "Auto-assign" to let DoIt distribute unassigned tasks across days. Use "Schedule my day" to push tasks to Google Calendar as events.

8. **Settings** — Configure working hours (start/end times), daily task budget (minutes), connect Google Calendar, set up recurring tasks, and enable the daily digest email.
